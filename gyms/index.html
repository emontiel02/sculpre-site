More than check-ins. What happens on the floor.

Your gym software already counts visits. Sculprē adds what members train, whether they’re progressing toward their goal, and who wants help.

Training clients from stuck members

Members still coming but no longer getting stronger get one question: want help from your trainers? A yes is emailed to you with their goal and what’s stuck.

Your own offers

Write an offer, like a free form check or a plateau class, and pick who sees it: members who are stuck or whose effort is dropping. It shows on their workout summary.

An earlier warning

Members who cut their sets for three weeks while still showing up. Your offer reaches them before their visits fall, when it’s still easy to win them back.

Results you can show

Typical strength gains at three and six months, and body results from members who opt in to share them. Copy them straight into your marketing.

Referrals you can count

Members’ new-best cards carry your gym’s name and code. See how many were shared and how many people joined through them.

Trainers, equipment and waits

Which trainers’ clients follow their programs and progress, what members use most against their goals, and where they likely wait at peak hours.

Your members’ data stays theirs.

Members choose to count their workouts toward your gym. You see totals, and a name only when a member asks to hear from you.

Totals of five or more

Any number fewer than five members make up is left out, so no one can be picked out.

Names only when a member asks

A member’s name reaches you when they say yes, tap your offer, or ask to be kept accountable.

Never food, nothing from Oura

Food logs are never shared, and body results come only from members who opt in to share them.

How it works.

  1. Become a partner gym

    We set up your gym and its code. When a member who joined with your code pays for Pro, you earn 20% of Sculprē’s share for as long as they subscribe.

  2. Members train as usual

    They log in Sculprē and choose to count toward your gym. Nothing extra to tap at the gym.

  3. Sign in to your dashboard

    We email you a link to set your password, then you sign in on a computer and your gym opens. Leads arrive by email the day a member asks.
