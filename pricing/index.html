Billing questions

How is Pro billed?

Through your Apple account, like any App Store subscription. You can cancel anytime on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sculprē Pro.

Will I be reminded before a trial ends?

Yes. Sculprē shows the trial’s dates before you start (today, a reminder two days before it ends, and the day the first charge comes) and sends that reminder.

Can my trainer pay for my Pro?

Yes. Trainers on Coach include Pro for five clients and can cover more. If yours does, Pro turns on in your app with nothing to buy.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing. Your food, workouts and body data stay on your phone and in the free app. Pro features simply turn off.
