Most apps see one part of your day. Sculprē sees all of it.

Separate apps can’t see that you slept five hours or skipped leg day. Sculprē can, so your food, lifts and sleep change each other’s plans.

READINESS

A short night eases the lifts

Readiness reads sleep, heart-rate variability and resting heart rate from Apple Health or Oura, plus yesterday’s eating and your pre-workout check-in. On a rough day, lift suggestions go easier.

SMART MACROS · PRO

A skipped workout trims the carbs

Rest days and missed workouts take a share off that day’s calories, from carbs. A day well over is evened out over the next one. Protein never moves.

CYCLE TRACKING

Targets follow your cycle

Turn on cycle tracking and the luteal phase adds about 125 calories a day as carbs, with readiness noting the phase. Periods can come in from Apple Health.

Nutrition

Logging that keeps up with real meals.

A calorie and macro tracker that sets your targets from your body and goal, with protein set for building or keeping muscle.

  • Scan, search or snap a photo. Barcodes, USDA whole foods, or a photo of your plate: 7 a week free, up to 12 a day with Pro.
  • The units you cook with. ¾ cup, a can, a slice or 4 oz, as fractions or decimals.
  • Recipes for meal prep. Scan or search each ingredient once, then log a portion with one tap.
  • Close out the day. In the evening, Sculprē suggests foods that fit what’s left, protein first.

Smart macros · Pro

Targets that move with your week.

  • Rest days eat a little less. 10% off unless you change it, from carbs (fat on keto). Training days keep your targets, since your activity level already counts them.
  • Skipping a workout today? Flip one switch on Today and the day comes down at once.
  • Over yesterday, even today. Pick how much of a big day to take back. No day moves more than about 15%, and calories never go under your floor.
  • Protein never moves. And any day can be undone.
