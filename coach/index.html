Built for how trainers actually work.

Programs in minutes

Write a week in the grid, start from a template, or send every client’s week from one spreadsheet. Clients start each day from the Train tab.

Macro targets by day

Set calories and macros for lifting, cardio and rest days. Clients choose to use them in place of their own.

Messages

Talk with each client in the app. Every message is sealed for the two of you before it leaves the device.

The week at a glance

Flags for the week, averages, and a row per day with training, calories and protein against target, sleep and check-ins. Each lift’s best set against earlier weeks.

Spreadsheets welcome

Build programs in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, one tab per client, and upload the workbook. Each tab is matched to a client by name.

Cardio in the plan

Add runs, rides and classes to a day with minutes, distance and effort. Clients log them right from that day’s workout.

Your clients’ data stays theirs.

Each day is sealed on the client’s phone for you alone. Sculprē’s servers keep only sealed copies they can’t read, and nothing is sold.

Clients choose what you see

Training, food diary, body and recovery are separate choices, and a client can change them or stop sharing anytime.

Your key lives on your phone

The key that opens your clients’ days is kept in your iPhone’s Keychain. Linking your computer hands it a copy, sealed for that browser.

Programs and messages too

Programs, templates and messages are sealed the same way before they leave the device.
